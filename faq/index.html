Frequently asked questions

No question matches. Email support@akhibrowser.com, and we'll help you.

What is AkhiBrowser?

A free browser for Muslims and families, built on Firefox. It blurs images and videos until they're checked, blocks haram sites and searches, and removes background music from videos. The filtering happens on your own device.

Is AkhiBrowser free?

Yes. The browser and its protections are free. Premium and Family Premium add extra features; see the prices.

Which devices does it work on?

Windows (Microsoft Store or the installer), Android phones and tablets (Google Play), and as an extension for Chrome and Firefox. There is no iPhone or Mac version yet.

Do you see what I browse?

No. Images and pages are checked on your device, and we keep no browsing history. To block adult sites, site names are checked with Cloudflare's family filter. See the privacy page.

What's the difference between Premium and Family Premium?

Premium lets you choose who is blurred (with hijab recognition) and adds strict mode, prayer pause and YouTube without Shorts. Family Premium adds the parent controls: a parental PIN, blocking sites and words, time limits and schedules, blocked apps on Android and a weekly report for parents, on up to 10 devices.

How do I turn on Premium?

In AkhiBrowser, open the menu, Extensions, AkhiBrowser, then Premium. Paste your key and press Activate. Bought in Google Play? Then Premium turns on by itself.

What happens after I pay?

You see your license key on the screen, and get it by email straight away. Paste it in AkhiBrowser under Premium. Family Premium by card starts with 3 days free, and a yearly plan gets an email a few days before it renews. When your key starts working on another device, we email you, so you notice if someone else uses it.

I bought on Android. Can I use it on my computer too?

Yes. In AkhiBrowser's Premium settings, get your key or have it emailed to you, and paste it in AkhiBrowser on Windows.

On how many devices does one key work?

A key works on 3 devices, a Family key on 10. When a key is full, a new device takes the place of the one used longest ago.

How do I cancel?

Bought on this website: under Premium, press Manage subscription. Bought in Google Play: in the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, AkhiBrowser.

I lost my key. What now?

Look for the email "Your AkhiBrowser Premium license key". Can't find it? Email support@akhibrowser.com from the address you paid with, and we'll send it again. Bought in Google Play? Your phone needs no key, and AkhiBrowser's Premium settings give you one for your computer.

Can my child turn the protections off?

With a parental PIN, settings can only be changed with the PIN. With the parent report on, you get an email when a protection is turned off. AkhiBrowser can't stop someone from uninstalling it; if a device stops reporting, you're emailed after 8 days.

Can I block apps like Instagram or TikTok?

On Android, with Family Premium: under Blocked apps, tick the apps and save with your PIN. A blocked app shows a message instead, and stays blocked after it's reinstalled.

How does the parent report work?

With Family Premium or a School / Mosque key, turn on Parent report by email in AkhiBrowser's settings and confirm the email we send. You then get an email when a protection or the PIN is turned off, and a weekly summary of blocked pages by reason, without page addresses. If a device stops reporting for 8 days, we let you know. Every email has a link to stop.

Is there a plan for schools and mosques?

Yes: the School / Mosque plan has everything in Family Premium, for up to 500 devices, to give to their families for free. See recommend to your school or mosque.

How do the families of our school or mosque get it?

After paying, you get your key on the screen and by email. 1. Try AkhiBrowser yourself first. 2. Share the key in your WhatsApp group or newsletter: families paste it in AkhiBrowser, on a computer or from Google Play, on up to 500 devices together. 3. The kit makes a ready message and a poster. Share the key only with your members, never publicly.

Can I give AkhiBrowser as a gift?

Yes, a year of Family Premium, which doesn't renew. On this website, the key is emailed to the person you give it to, with a copy to you. In AkhiBrowser, you get the key to pass on yourself. It works on Windows and on Android.

Are the filters perfect?

No filter catches everything. AkhiBrowser makes the internet much safer, but it doesn't replace a parent's care.

How do I recommend AkhiBrowser and earn from it?

Fill in the form on recommend to your school or mosque. Once we approve it, you get your code and links by email, and a form for your IBAN. A school or mosque that subscribes through your link earns you 20% of each payment, for as long as it stays. Influencers in our VIP program get 10% of each Family payment, and their followers 10% off. Each month we email what you earned, and transfer it.

How do I become a sponsor or partner?

Fill in the form on how we make money. We read every request and answer within a few days. Once approved, you get a welcome email with a short form for your logo, link and invoice details. Ticked the box to be listed? Then your organisation appears on our sponsors page. A partner never gets our users' data, or a say in what is blocked.

What do I get from the newsletter?

A verse of the Quran and what's new, every Friday. You confirm your address first. A week later you get 10% off Family Premium once, if you haven't bought it yet. Every email has a link to stop. Sign up.

How do I delete my data?

AkhiBrowser has no account, so there's nothing about you to delete with us. Your history and settings stay on your device. For a Mozilla account, purchases in Google Play and the parent report, see delete your data.

Is paying safe?

Payments on this website go through Stripe: your card details go to Stripe, never to us, and you can also pay once with iDEAL or PayPal. You don't need an account. In AkhiBrowser from Google Play, Google takes the payment. AkhiBrowser is registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK 82581509).

Where can I leave a review?

Thank you! A review helps other families find AkhiBrowser: on Google Play, the Microsoft Store, or for the extension in the Chrome Web Store or on Firefox Add-ons. Something not right? Email us first, and we'll fix it.

How do I contact you?

Email support@akhibrowser.com. We answer in English, Dutch and Arabic.
