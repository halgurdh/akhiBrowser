Tips for parents: close the gaps

AkhiBrowser protects what happens in AkhiBrowser. A child who opens another browser or app gets around it. These steps close those gaps, and most take a few minutes.

  • Give your child their own account

    On Windows, a standard account, not an administrator, so your child can't install other browsers or change settings. On Android, a Google account for your child, managed with Google Family Link.

  • Make AkhiBrowser the default browser

    Links from other apps then open in AkhiBrowser. Windows: Settings, Apps, Default apps. Android: Settings, Apps, Default apps, Browser app.

  • Block the other browsers

    On Android, block Chrome and any other browser in Family Link, under your child's app limits. On Windows, uninstall the browsers your child doesn't need, and block the rest in Microsoft Family Safety, under apps and games.

  • Filter the whole phone or home network

    AkhiBrowser looks up sites through Cloudflare's family DNS. Use it for every app too: on Android, set Private DNS to family.cloudflare-dns.com (Settings, Network and internet; the path differs a little per phone). On your router, set the DNS servers to 1.1.1.3 and 1.0.0.3, for every device on your Wi-Fi.

  • Set the parent PIN

    In AkhiBrowser's settings, set a parent PIN, so the protections can't be turned off without you.

  • Talk with your child

    No filter catches everything. Tell your child why these rules are there, and that they can always come to you when they see something wrong.
