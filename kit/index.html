With the School / Mosque plan, your school or mosque gives AkhiBrowser Premium to its families for free: one key for up to 500 devices. This page writes the message and the poster for you.

  • 1. Get the key

    Take the School / Mosque plan, €30 a month, which you can cancel at any time. You get the key on the screen and by email.

  • 2. Share it with your families

    Paste the key below for a ready message for your WhatsApp group or newsletter, and a poster to print. The key stays on this page: nothing is sent to us.

  • 3. Families install AkhiBrowser

    They download AkhiBrowser, paste the key under Premium, and set it up with the tips for parents.
