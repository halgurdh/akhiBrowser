How we make money

AkhiBrowser is free, shows no ads, and never sells or shares what you do. This is how it pays for itself, every way of it:

  • Premium

    Optional paid features for you, for families, and for schools and mosques. Most of the money comes from here.

  • Search partners

    When a search engine has a partner contract with us, a search you start in AkhiBrowser carries our partner code, and the engine gives us a small share of what it earns. Only the code is added: nothing about you or your search comes to us.

  • Partner shops, only if you choose

    Off unless you turn it on: when you go to a partner shop yourself, we add our code and the shop gives us a small share. You pay the same, and a link from another site keeps its own code.

  • Tips on our start page

    Now and then a tip with an affiliate link, always labelled as one. We never change the links of other websites.

  • Affiliates, sponsors and partners

    People who bring in schools and mosques get 20% of their payments. Sponsors and organisations can support AkhiBrowser, but never get your data or a say in what is blocked.

What we never do: show ads, sell or share your browsing, track you across sites, or let money change what is blocked.
