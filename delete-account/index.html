Delete your account or data

This page is for AkhiBrowser, the halal browser for Windows and Android, made by Halgurd Hussein (AkhiBrowser). AkhiBrowser has no account of its own: you never sign up with us, and we keep no personal data about you to delete.

A Mozilla account, made for Sync

AkhiBrowser is built on Firefox, and keeps Firefox Sync. If you made a Mozilla account in AkhiBrowser to sync your bookmarks and passwords, that account is held by Mozilla, not by us. To delete it and its synced data:

  1. Go to accounts.firefox.com and sign in.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and press Delete account.
  3. Confirm, and enter your password. The account and its synced data are deleted for good.

Mozilla's own steps: How do I delete my Mozilla account? To only stop syncing, sign out of Sync in AkhiBrowser's settings.

Premium, license keys and purchases

  • Bought on Google Play: Google holds the purchase. Cancel it in the Play Store, under your profile picture, Payments and subscriptions.
  • A license key: it isn't an account. Our license server keeps only hashes of the key and of a random install id, no name or email. A device you stop using frees its place after 30 days, and its hash is removed.
  • Parent emails (Family): turn the report off in AkhiBrowser's settings, or use the stop link in any of its emails. Your address is then removed.

Data on your device

Your history, settings and the activity summary stay on your device. Uninstalling AkhiBrowser deletes them, or clear them in the browser's settings.

Questions, or something you want removed? Email support@akhibrowser.com. More in the privacy policy.
