The best halal browsers in 2026, compared

2026-11-10

A halal browser blocks haram sites, forces SafeSearch and keeps unwanted images off your screen. In 2026 there are three active options worth knowing. We make one of them, AkhiBrowser, so this page sticks to what each browser states on its own pages, and says plainly where each one fits best.

The short answer

  • On Windows, or for parent controls: AkhiBrowser. Free protections, background music removal, and Family Premium with a PIN, site lists and time limits.
  • On a Mac, or for an all-free browser on every platform: Kahf Browser. Windows, macOS, Android and iOS, free with no in-app purchases.
  • On an iPhone: Tahir Browser or Kahf Browser. AkhiBrowser isn't on iOS yet.

AkhiBrowser

A halal browser built on Firefox, for Windows now and Android soon. Adult, alcohol, drug, vaping and gambling sites are blocked from the first page, SafeSearch is forced on Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo and YouTube, and images stay blurred until they're checked on your device. It's the only one of the three that removes background music from videos, on your device. The protections are free; Premium adds the choice of who is blurred, and Family Premium adds a parental PIN, site lists, time limits and a pause button.

Kahf Browser

A Chromium-based browser for Muslims on Windows, macOS, Android and iOS. Its site says it is free on all platforms with no subscriptions or in-app purchases, blocks ads, adult and haram sites with AI filtering and a database, enforces SafeSearch and Safe YouTube, blurs nudity in real time, and includes prayer times and the qibla. Most Chrome extensions work in it.

Tahir Browser

An iPhone and iPad browser by Ihya Tech. Its App Store listing describes blurring all images or using AI to blur the opposite gender, tap-to-unblur, blocking haram sites, ads and trackers, Islamic reminders, forced SafeSearch and a password or biometric lock on its shield. It's free to download, with optional monthly and yearly plans.

What happened to SalamWeb?

SalamWeb was one of the first halal browsers and got wide press coverage in 2019. It was discontinued in 2021 and its website is gone, so it's no longer an option. It's a good reminder to choose a browser that's still updated, because updates bring security fixes.
