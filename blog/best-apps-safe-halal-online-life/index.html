The 10 best apps for a safe, halal online life in 2027

2026-11-24

A safe internet for your family takes more than one app. AkhiBrowser blocks harmful content, and these ten tools cover the rest: your email, your passwords, your money and your learning.

  1. Proton Mail: Encrypted email and cloud storage from Switzerland. Why you'd want it: your email and files stay private, without ads reading along.
  2. NordPass: One strong password for everything else, with safe sharing in the family. Why you'd want it: no more reused passwords that get hacked.
  3. Dashlane: A password manager that warns you when your data shows up in a leak. Why you'd want it: you know about a leak before someone misuses it.
  4. Zoya: Check if a stock is halal before you invest. Why you'd want it: you invest with a clear conscience.
  5. Wahed: Halal investing with Shariah-compliant portfolios and no riba. Why you'd want it: your savings grow without interest.
  6. Darussalam: Islamic books for every age: Quran translations, hadith and children's stories. Why you'd want it: trusted knowledge for the whole family, at home.
  7. Shukr: Modest, high-quality Islamic clothing for men and women. Why you'd want it: modest clothes that last and look good.
  8. Amazon: Prayer mats, Islamic gifts and children's books, delivered fast. Why you'd want it: everything for Ramadan and Eid in one order.
  9. Skillshare: Online classes in design, writing, calligraphy and more. Why you'd want it: learn something useful together, instead of scrolling.
  10. AkhiBrowser Family: Our own: real-time image and video blurring, a Qibla compass and parental controls with a PIN. Why you'd want it: your children browse safely, without you watching every click.
