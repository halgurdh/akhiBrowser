Which one fits you?

Kahf Browser fits if you use an iPhone or a Mac, or want everything free.

AkhiBrowser fits if you want background music removed, want to choose who is blurred, or need parent controls such as a PIN and time limits, on Windows or Android.

Both are free to try, so the best test is your own use. AkhiBrowser isn't affiliated with Kahf. Spotted something out of date? Tell us at support@akhibrowser.com and we'll correct it.
