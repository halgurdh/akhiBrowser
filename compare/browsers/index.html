In short

Brave, Firefox and Chrome are good general browsers, but none of them filters haram content or blurs images by itself. You would need extensions, which a child can remove.

Kahf Browser and AkhiBrowser are made for Muslims and filter from the start. AkhiBrowser adds background music removal, choosing who to blur, and parent controls with a PIN.
